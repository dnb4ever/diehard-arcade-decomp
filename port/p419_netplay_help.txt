Online co-op (Netplay tab)

Host (player 1): choose the UDP port, default 7845. The app tries automatic
PCP/NAT-PMP/UPnP mapping with a short renewable lease and shows the external
IPv4:port on success. Copy that endpoint; your friend selects Join by IP.
On the same LAN, share the LAN endpoint. Both press Start. If automatic mapping
is unavailable, internet hosting may still need UDP forwarding in the router.
Mappings are removed on normal exit and expire after a crash.

Join by IP (player 2): type or paste the host's numeric IPv4:port and press Start.

Connect by code: both choose Generate / refresh my code, copy and exchange
codes, then paste the OTHER person's code. Both press Start. Player 1 / 2 is
assigned automatically. Codes use LAN and public STUN/router candidates and
simultaneous UDP hole punching. Exchange fresh codes after refreshing/restarting.
Symmetric NAT / CGNAT can still require UDP forwarding; no relay is provided.
Connection settings can disable mapping/STUN or change the two STUN servers
(defaults stun.l.google.com:19302 and stun.cloudflare.com:3478).

Use matching P4.19 builds, ROMs and input delay (default 3 fields). Both people
use their local P1 bindings; the guest drives cabinet P2. Host game settings
and 1x rendering apply. Rewind, quick states, movies, turbo, cheats, pause and
speed changes are locked during a session. Close the window to end play.
Netplay audio defaults to Low latency: unplayed rollback differences are
corrected with a short fade. Glitch-free retains the longer confirmed-only
buffer. Poor connections or insufficient CPU can still cause stalls.

CLI flags: --netplay-host 7845, --netplay-join HOST_IPV4:7845,
--netplay-code 7845 --netplay-peer-code DHA1-PEERS_CODE,
--input-delay 0..3, --netplay-audio low-latency|glitch-free,
--netplay-no-map, --netplay-no-stun, --netplay-stun-servers HOST:PORT,HOST:PORT.
Codes are not encryption. Share your endpoint/code with the intended player.
Local fixture tests pass; real-router/internet, speakers and native Mac/Windows
P4.19 acceptance remain owner checks. See notes/port/p419_netplay_polish.md.
