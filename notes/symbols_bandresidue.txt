## Symbol correction (do NOT edit notes/symbols.txt per task; recorded here)
0x02213234 sh2_fxdiv_16_16   # CORRECTION: notes/symbols.txt lists this as "sh2_hw_divide num/den". It is actually a 16.16 fixed-point divide: returns ((s64)num << 16) / den via the on-chip DIVU (DVSR=den, dividend = (s64)num<<16, reads DVDNTUL). num=r4, den=r5. Confirmed by disasm (swap.w+exts.w into DVDNTH, num<<16 into DVDNTL).
